Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 16, 2026 · App version 1.0.0 · Developer: Jongwon Yi (individual developer) · Contact: jongwyi@gmail.com

  • FolderGolder does not collect any personal data. The developer receives no data from the app.
  • There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no crash-reporting tools. The app contains no third-party SDKs.
  • The developer runs no servers. Everything the app saves stays on your Mac.
  • The app connects to the internet only to search a public icon service (Iconify) when you ask it to, and to show icon websites in its built-in browser.

1. Data the developer collects

None. FolderGolder does not send any information about you, your Mac, your folders or your use of the app to the developer or to any party chosen by the developer.

2. Data stored on your Mac

The app saves the following data only on your Mac, inside its sandbox container: ~/Library/Containers/com.foldergolder.app/Data/Library/Application Support/FolderGolder/

ItemWhat it containsWhy
library/Images you imported, downloaded or drew, plus index.json with each image's name, source address and licence noteYour icon Library
history.json, history-previous/Which folders received which icon, and copies of their previous iconsHistory, Reset and Restore Previous

The app also keeps its settings in the standard macOS preferences for the app (UserDefaults). These include your settings, recently used emoji, hidden icon sets, which websites are shown in the browser, and whether the "copy to Library" setting is on.

The built-in browser keeps website data (cookies, cache and site storage) inside the same container, as any web browser does, so you can stay signed in to icon sites you use.

3. Your folders

FolderGolder reads the names of the folders you add to suggest matching icons. This matching runs entirely on your Mac. Folder names are never sent anywhere.

The app changes only folders you choose, by dragging them into the window or selecting them with Add Folders. For each one, it writes a custom folder icon (the standard Finder custom icon) and updates the folder's modification date so Finder shows the change. Reset removes the custom icon again.

4. Network connections

Iconify (icon search)

When you run a search (by pressing Return in a search field) or browse Iconify icon sets, the app sends a request to the public Iconify API at https://api.iconify.design. The request contains your search words and the names of the icons to load. Like every internet request, it also carries your IP address and a basic app identifier ("FolderGolder (macOS)"). Iconify is an independent service that the developer does not operate or control. Its own privacy policy applies.

Icon suggestions based on folder names never use the network.

Built-in browser

The Browse view is a web browser for icon websites. It includes about 35 selected icon sites and any site you add or type into the address bar. When you open a site, that site receives the same information any browser sends, such as your IP address, and may set cookies. These sites operate independently. Their own terms and privacy policies apply. The developer does not receive any of this data.

Files you download in the browser are added to your Library.

Copy to Library

When the "copy to Library" setting is on (Settings › Web Sources, "Add images I copy in the browser to my Library") and you copy an image or an image address inside the built-in browser, the app saves that image to your Library. If an address was copied, the app downloads the image from that address using a private connection without cookies.

The app checks for copied content only while the built-in browser is visible and this setting is on. Anything you copy in other apps is never imported.

Emoji

Emoji are drawn on your Mac with the fonts built into macOS. Nothing is downloaded for them.

5. How data is used

Data on your Mac is used only to provide the app's features: showing your Library, applying and restoring folder icons, remembering your settings and speeding up searches. It is never used for advertising, profiling or tracking, and it is never sold or shared by the developer.

6. Security

Network requests use standard HTTPS provided by macOS. The app runs in the macOS App Sandbox and can reach only folders you select. Files you download in the browser are saved inside the app's own container.

7. Keeping and deleting data

Data stays on your Mac until you delete it. You can:

  • delete items from the Library in the app;
  • reset folder icons with Reset (History & Reset);
  • remove websites you added in Settings › Web Sources;
  • remove all app data, including browser cookies and cache, by quitting the app and deleting ~/Library/Containers/com.foldergolder.app. Deleting the app and this folder removes everything the app stored.

Because the developer holds no data about you, there is nothing to request, correct or delete from the developer.

8. Your choices and consent

  • Iconify requests happen only when you search or browse icon sets. Not searching means no requests are made.
  • You choose which websites to open. You can hide sites in Settings › Web Sources.
  • You can turn "copy to Library" off at any time in Settings › Web Sources.
  • You can revoke the app's access to a folder by resetting its icon and removing it from the app, or by deleting the app's container as described above.

9. Children

FolderGolder is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect data from anyone.

10. Third-party icons and licences

Icons on third-party websites belong to their creators. The app shows each site's licence note and saves it with every imported image. You are responsible for following the licence of any icon you download and use.

11. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at the same address with a new effective date. Material changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.

12. Contact

앱은 사용자가 창으로 끌어다 놓거나 Add Folders로 선택한 폴더만 변경합니다. 각 폴더에 사용자 지정 폴더 아이콘(Finder의 표준 사용자 지정 아이콘)을 기록하고, Finder가 변경 사항을 바로 보여 주도록 폴더의 수정 날짜를 갱신합니다. 초기화(Reset)를 하면 사용자 지정 아이콘이 다시 제거됩니다.

Iconify (아이콘 검색)

사용자가 검색 칸에서 Return 키를 눌러 검색을 실행하거나 Iconify 아이콘 세트를 둘러볼 때, 앱은 공개 Iconify API(https://api.iconify.design)에 요청을 보냅니다. 요청에는 검색어와 불러올 아이콘 이름이 포함됩니다. 모든 인터넷 요청과 마찬가지로 IP 주소와 기본적인 앱 식별 정보("FolderGolder (macOS)")도 함께 전달됩니다. Iconify는 개발자가 운영하거나 관리하지 않는 독립된 서비스이며, Iconify 자체의 개인정보 처리방침이 적용됩니다.

폴더 이름을 바탕으로 한 아이콘 추천은 네트워크를 전혀 사용하지 않습니다.

내장 브라우저

Browse 화면은 아이콘 웹사이트를 위한 웹 브라우저입니다. 약 35개의 엄선된 아이콘 사이트와 사용자가 직접 추가하거나 주소창에 입력한 사이트를 열 수 있습니다. 사이트를 열면 해당 사이트는 일반 브라우저와 마찬가지로 IP 주소 등의 정보를 받으며 쿠키를 설정할 수 있습니다. 이러한 사이트는 독립적으로 운영되며, 각 사이트의 이용약관과 개인정보 처리방침이 적용됩니다. 개발자는 이 데이터를 전혀 받지 않습니다.

앱은 내장 브라우저가 화면에 표시되어 있고 이 설정이 켜져 있는 동안에만 복사된 내용을 확인합니다. 다른 앱에서 복사한 내용은 절대 가져오지 않습니다.

FAQ

The icon did not change in Finder.
Finder caches icons. Wait a few seconds, then close and reopen the Finder window. If the folder is on the Desktop, click elsewhere and back. The app updates the folder's modification date so Finder notices the change, but Finder may still take a moment.
How do I go back to the normal folder icon?
Open History & Reset and choose Reset, or select the folders and reset them. To bring back the icon a folder had before FolderGolder changed it, use Restore Previous Icon.
Where does FolderGolder store its data?
Only on your Mac, in ~/Library/Containers/com.foldergolder.app/Data/Library/Application Support/FolderGolder/. This holds your Library, History, folder bookmarks, websites you added and the Iconify cache. Browser cookies and cache are kept in the same container.
How do I remove FolderGolder completely?
  1. Reset any folder icons you want to return to normal.
  2. Quit FolderGolder and move it to the Trash.
  3. Delete ~/Library/Containers/com.foldergolder.app (in Finder, choose Go › Go to Folder).
Folders keep their custom icons until you reset them, even after the app is removed. You can also remove a custom icon in Finder's Get Info window.
Can I use icons I download from websites?
Each icon belongs to its creator. FolderGolder shows each site's licence note and saves it with the image in your Library. Please follow that licence. You are responsible for how you use downloaded icons.
Does it work offline?
Yes. Suggestions, the bundled icons, styles, colours, Pixel Draw, your Library, Apply and Reset all work offline. Iconify search and the built-in browser need an internet connection.
Does FolderGolder send my folder names anywhere?
No. Suggestions are made on your Mac. See the Privacy Policy.

자주 묻는 질문

Finder에서 아이콘이 바뀌지 않습니다.
Finder는 아이콘을 캐시에 저장해 둡니다. 몇 초 기다린 뒤 Finder 창을 닫았다가 다시 열어 주십시오. 바탕화면의 폴더라면 다른 곳을 클릭했다가 다시 선택해 보십시오. 앱은 Finder가 변경을 알아차리도록 폴더의 수정 날짜를 갱신하지만, 반영까지 잠시 시간이 걸릴 수 있습니다.
원래 폴더 아이콘으로 되돌리려면 어떻게 하나요?
History & Reset에서 Reset을 선택하거나, 폴더를 선택한 뒤 초기화합니다. FolderGolder가 바꾸기 전의 아이콘을 되살리려면 Restore Previous Icon을 사용합니다.
데이터는 어디에 저장되나요?
사용자의 Mac에만 저장되며, 위치는 ~/Library/Containers/com.foldergolder.app/Data/Library/Application Support/FolderGolder/입니다. 여기에는 보관함, 기록, 폴더 북마크, 직접 추가한 웹사이트, Iconify 캐시가 들어 있습니다. 브라우저 쿠키와 캐시도 같은 컨테이너에 보관됩니다.
FolderGolder를 완전히 삭제하려면 어떻게 하나요?
  1. 원래대로 되돌리고 싶은 폴더 아이콘을 먼저 초기화합니다.
  2. FolderGolder를 종료하고 휴지통으로 옮깁니다.
  3. ~/Library/Containers/com.foldergolder.app 폴더를 삭제합니다(Finder에서 이동 › 폴더로 이동).
앱을 삭제해도 폴더의 사용자 지정 아이콘은 초기화하기 전까지 그대로 남습니다. Finder의 정보 가져오기 창에서도 사용자 지정 아이콘을 제거할 수 있습니다.
웹사이트에서 내려받은 아이콘을 써도 되나요?
각 아이콘의 권리는 제작자에게 있습니다. FolderGolder는 사이트별 라이선스 안내를 보여 주고, 보관함의 이미지와 함께 저장합니다. 해당 라이선스를 꼭 지켜 주십시오. 내려받은 아이콘의 사용에 대한 책임은 사용자에게 있습니다.
오프라인에서도 사용할 수 있나요?
네. 추천, 기본 제공 아이콘, 스타일, 색상, Pixel Draw, 보관함, 적용과 초기화는 모두 오프라인에서 작동합니다. Iconify 검색과 내장 브라우저만 인터넷 연결이 필요합니다.
폴더 이름이 외부로 전송되나요?
아닙니다. 추천은 Mac 안에서만 이루어집니다. 자세한 내용은 개인정보 처리방침을 참고해 주십시오.
